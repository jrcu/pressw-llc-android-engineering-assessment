<b>Scope committed:</b>
1. AI Assistance that answers cooking questions and suggest recipes.
2. Suggest recipes based on what the user wants.
3. Answers questions in under 2s.
4. Doesn't assume what cooking utensils or cookware the user has.
5. Optimized for unit economics so that every conversation doesn't come with a high price without sacrificing user experience.
6. It should refuse to answer things that aren't about cooking.

<b>Scope cut:</b>
1. Data retention. For v1, we will not add retention of food preferences, allergens and cookware or utensils.
2. Support for users under the age of 13.
3. Hands Free support. We can add support for answers to be read out loud on v2.

<b>Contradictions resolved:</b>
1. Response time. Marcus is ok with a longer response time to ensure the responses are good.

<b>Clarifying questions:</b>
1. What does constitute as medical questions when it comes to recipes due to dietary restrictions such as gluten free for users with gluten allergies.

<b>Assumptions made:</b>
1. We are assuming that answering "No medical, dietary, or therapeutic advice" as stated by Diane falls under the 6th requirement as stated above.

<b>Risks accepted:</b>
1. Due to amount of time the response must come we are accepting the risk of answers not being optimized for the conversation such as correctness.
2. Memory is a big one here. It has been stated by several stakeholders that it is something users are asking for. Due to the timeline we are going to cut it for now but build with it in mind for the future.
